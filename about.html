01 — Background

From Biology to Engineering

My name is Hassan Ahmed Hasan Al-Nahari, and I'm a first-year BS Artificial Intelligence Engineering student at Mapúa University.

I studied Biology before moving to AI Engineering. I enjoyed learning about research and laboratory work, but I gradually realized I was more interested in numbers, calculations, and logical problems. Engineering was something I'd been curious about for a long time, so I decided to give it a serious try.

Changing programs wasn't about forgetting what I learned in Biology. It was about finding a direction that fits my interests better.

02 — My choice

Why AI Engineering?

I chose AI Engineering because artificial intelligence is becoming part of almost everything we use online. We encounter it in search engines, websites, apps, and services that help people make decisions or complete everyday tasks.

What interests me most is what's happening behind the screen: the mathematics, data, and algorithms that make those systems work. I enjoy solving problems, and I want to understand how an AI system is built rather than only use one.

My background in Biology might also become useful in the future, particularly when studying how AI can support healthcare or biological research.

03 — Looking forward

What I'm working toward

Right now, my priorities are learning mathematics and programming, improving the way I approach problems, and getting comfortable with engineering coursework.

As I progress, I hope to explore machine learning, data analysis, and software development. I'm not claiming to know everything about AI yet. I want to build a strong foundation first and get better through practice.

I also want to become someone who asks better questions, works well in a team, and checks their results carefully instead of assuming the first answer is correct.
